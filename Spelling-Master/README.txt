SPELLING MASTER

Open index.html in a modern browser, or upload this complete Spelling-Master folder to a static host such as Netlify. Keep index.html, the JavaScript/CSS files and assets together. No build command is needed; the publish directory is the folder containing index.html.

Choose one of 20 school-supply pictures. Use that same picture to continue a child's work. No student name, password or ID is required. The selected object's name appears throughout the activities and on every assessment row. On a shared device, assign children different objects.

BUILD A WORD
A, B, C use 3 correct spelling chunks plus 1 extra distractor letter: 4 tiles in the mixed bank.
AA, BB, CCC use 4 correct spelling chunks plus 3 extra distractor letters: 7 tiles in the mixed bank.
Use the correct chunks to build the word and leave the extra letters in the tray. Distractors are separate single letters, mixed in with the answer chunks. Existing saved work keeps its placed answer tiles and receives the extra letters in its tray.
Listen to a picture sentence and the word to spell. Build the missing word. Letter teams, initial blends, vowel neighbours, doubled consonants and common endings stay together where applicable; some tiles contain larger word parts. A tile is not necessarily one phoneme.
As each piece is placed, the student's current spelling appears in the sentence's blank. Reordering, returning pieces to the tray, mixing again and resuming saved work all update the blank. Incorrect combinations are shown as entered and are checked only with Check my answer.

BUILD A SENTENCE
A: 3–4 words; B: 5–6; C: 7–8; AA: 9–10; BB: 11–14; CCC: 15–22.
Listen and reconstruct the complete sentence. Capital letters and punctuation stay attached to their words. Insert pieces before, between or after other pieces, reorder them, or move them back to the tray.
Sentence word tiles use grey text in the bank and black text when placed in the answer area. Returning a tile to the bank makes its text grey again. A little extra space separates the sentence word bank from the answer area.
The sentence appears above the pieces as the student builds it, in the exact order chosen. Adding, reordering, removing, resetting or resuming words updates this live sentence. Unplaced words and the correct answer are not supplied automatically.

RESET QUESTION
The visible Reset question button beside Check my answer works in both activities at every level. It clears the student's answer, returns all pieces to the mixed bank, hides the hint and stops speech. The student stays on the same question. Previous assessment records, completion, support ratings and hint/listen counts are kept. Teacher tools has a separate confirmed reset for all progress belonging to the selected school supply.

YOUR NOTEBOOK
Both activity types use a lined notebook page with red margin lines and a pencil. The date at the top uses the format Sunday October 11th, 2026. It follows the student's device's current local date, refreshes at midnight and when returning to the project, and is not a fixed date saved with the activity.
The interface uses Century Gothic in upright type, including notebook text and the date. Visible + buttons let students insert pieces at the beginning, between pieces or at the end.
The activity measures the available screen height and uses compact spacing when needed to keep the question, pieces and controls on one screen. It rechecks after words move, hints open, or the screen is resized or rotated. Readable text and touch targets are retained; unusually small screens can still scroll rather than clipping the question.
Learning text and pieces are larger. Spelling pieces sit directly below the answer area, aligned beside the red notebook margin, so students can drag them straight up. This spelling area stays close together on taller screens. The sentence builder keeps its side-by-side answer and mixed-word bank on tablet and desktop screens, and uses spare screen height to enlarge its answer area. Narrow phone screens stack the two piece areas. The assessment download, support ratings, progress and full progress reset are in the Teacher tools menu.

TABLET AND KEYBOARD USE
Drag with a finger, stylus or mouse. Or tap/click a piece and then a + insertion point. Keyboard users can Tab to a piece, press Enter, then Tab to a + and press Enter. Return selected piece removes a tile to the tray. Pieces never overwrite or delete one another. A cancelled drag leaves the piece in place.
Tablet activities suppress pull-to-refresh gestures and native panning inside the draggable areas. The teacher menu retains normal scrolling, and multitouch zoom is allowed outside the piece areas. Browser toolbar refresh and operating-system controls remain under the browser's control; saved work is restored if the page is reloaded.

ASSESSMENT
Check my answer records the response, correctness and check number. Excel records the offered word bank and its distractor letters. Hints and listens are counted separately. Teachers may record Independent, With help or Practising. Download an actual Excel .xlsx workbook with Summary, Progress, Attempts and Guide sheets. The activity page downloads the selected object's assessment; the landing page downloads all 20 objects. There is no PDF assessment.

Progress is saved only in this browser on this device. Export Excel before clearing browser data, changing devices, or moving to a different site address. A download is a record, not a restore file. Two children who share an object on the same browser share that object's progress.

VOICE
Uses the device's built-in English text-to-speech voice. The first tap permits speech; use Listen if a browser blocks automatic playback. Available voices depend on the device. If voice is unavailable, a teacher can read the sentence or use the hint. Nothing is sent to a speech server by this application.

PICTURES AND VOCABULARY
Reuses the existing Mystic-Series, Magic Cat B and Magic Cat C material. Picture crops retain their native resolution. The 20 student selectors are original school-supply SVG illustrations. There are 498 spelling activities and 540 sentence-building activities (1,038 total).

Spelling bank: A has 48 simple CVC words; B, C, AA, BB and CCC each have 90 activities. Sentence bank: 90 activities in every level.

UNCOMPRESSED DELIVERY
No ZIP is needed. Upload the entire Spelling-Master folder, not index.html alone.
