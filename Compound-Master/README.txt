COMPOUND MASTER

Open index.html, or upload the complete Compound-Master folder to a static host. Keep index.html, all JavaScript/CSS files and assets together. No build command is required. Nothing has been published or zipped.

THE SAME ENTRY AND NOTEBOOK STRUCTURE
This is a new project based on Blend Master's layout and controls. Choose one of 20 school-supply pictures to identify saved work. No name, password or student ID is required. The selected school supply remains visible during activities and is included on assessment rows.
Compound Master has its own saved-progress key, separate from Blend Master, Spelling Master and Mystic/Magic Cat. The original projects are preserved.

COMPOUND WORD PARTS
Every answer button contains a component word, such as sun, flower, tooth or brush. Students complete a missing part of a compound word in a sentence. For example, __flower needs sun; sun__ needs flower. The missing part varies between the first and last part. The three-component practice example peanut butter also varies its middle part: pea + nut + butter. Written spaces in open compounds are preserved.
A, B, C: one blank and exactly three choices. Sentences increase in length through these levels.
AA: two blanks; BB: three blanks; CC: four blanks. These levels have exactly five reusable choices, with a missing part in each of several compound words. They mix missing first and last parts, and occasional middle parts.
There are 200 picture-based questions in each level: 1,200 activities in total, using 95 different compounds. Some compounds appear in more than one supplied picture. Additional compound words in the longer sentences provide a simple story around the main picture.

VOICE, ANSWERS AND NEXT BUTTONS
Listen reads only the target sentence, using the device's built-in English voice. There are no spoken instructions or reward sounds. Stop cancels speech. Slower, Steady and Faster settings are available. If automatic speech is blocked, tap Listen.
Drag a choice onto any blank, or tap a blank then a push-button choice. Keyboard users can Tab to a blank, press Enter, then Tab to a choice and press Enter. A correct choice selects the next unfinished blank. Incorrect choices remain visible so the student can try another.
Each choice stays available in the bank for reuse. Bank text is grey and placed text is black. Clear this blank removes the selected answer. Check my answer records a separate sentence check after all blanks are filled. Show me a hint reveals the full sentence and is counted in the assessment.
The separate MY JOINED WORDS display shows each assembled compound as the student taps or drags a part into its blank. Several blanks produce several whole-word displays. It shows the student's current choice, including a mistaken combination, rather than revealing the missing answer beforehand. Correct combinations have a pale green background. Normal spaces in open compounds such as hot dog and peanut butter are retained. Clearing a blank removes that joined word; reset and navigation update the display; saved answers restore it. Joining words does not trigger any extra speech.
There are Next buttons after the year in the date, beside the answer choices and at the bottom. They stay synchronized and disable at the end of a level.
Reset question clears current answers and the hint, and stops speech. It keeps all assessment records and cumulative achievements. Teacher tools includes a separate confirmed reset for all Compound Master progress belonging to the selected school supply.

TEXT BETWEEN NOTEBOOK LINES
The notebook keeps the red margin lines and pencil. Words and answer blanks share fixed-height writing rows. Blue ruling sits at the row boundaries, below the letters, and compound word pieces stay together within a written word. Text uses upright Century Gothic, with a sans-serif fallback if that font is unavailable.
The date follows the student's current local device date, for example Wednesday October 14th, 2026. It refreshes at midnight and when returning to the project.
Spacing adapts to screen height. Very small screens can scroll to keep text and touch targets usable. Actual browser/tablet appearance remains visually unverified because local preview is blocked in this session.

CANOE REWARD
Your supplied canoe picture has been turned into a transparent cutout. Children in life jackets travel from left to right across the water strip. The canoe gently bobs while it moves. Its side panel shows the selected school supply's cumulative number of questions first completed correctly across all six levels.
All four wooden paddles now rotate about their grip points while the canoe advances. They stop when the canoe rests. The original hands sit in front of the paddle layers. The canoe without paddles and the isolated paddle artwork are separate transparent assets; the original canoe.png remains available. Reduced-motion settings disable both bobbing and paddle motion.
One fully completed correct question earns one step. Individual blanks, wrong answers, repeated checks and redoing an already-completed question do not add extra steps. Every 20 correct questions finishes a journey; after a short pause the canoe returns to the left. The cumulative total keeps increasing.
The total is saved with the existing completion records. Each school supply has an independent total. Reset question preserves it; the full object-progress reset clears it. Motion respects the device's reduced-motion preference. No tractor or wheels are used.

TABLET USE AND SAVED PROGRESS
Finger, stylus and mouse dragging use Pointer Events. Pull-to-refresh gestures are suppressed in the activity, while teacher-menu scrolling remains available. Browser toolbar refresh and operating-system controls stay under browser control. Saved work restores after a reload.
Saved progress stays in this browser on this device. Export Excel before clearing browser data, changing devices or changing site addresses. An Excel export is a record, not a restore file. Two children choosing the same school supply in the same browser share its progress.

EXCEL ASSESSMENT
Teacher tools downloads a real .xlsx workbook for the selected school supply. The entry page can download all 20 objects. The workbook contains Summary, Progress, Blanks, Attempts and Guide sheets. There is no PDF assessment.
Each choice tap or successful drop records the school supply, time, level, activity, compound, missing-part position, offered choices, selected choice, correctness, current sentence and input method. The Blanks sheet includes the full compound, its missing first/middle/last part, current answer and attempts. Sentence checks, hints, listens, active time and teacher support ratings are recorded separately.

PICTURES AND CONTENT
Pictures are native crops from the three supplied word posters. The vowel-neighbour pages and non-compound entries such as turtle, penguin, window, train, ruler and scissors are excluded. The pictured cheeseburger and snowboard are used with those names. Open compounds use normal spaces, including hot dog, school bus, pencil case, beach ball and tape dispenser.
Printed answer captions are cropped away. Words inside the supermarket sign, vehicle signs, doormats and classroom boards are covered in the activity. Covers remain aligned when pictures resize. Picture crops are not enlarged beyond their native width.
