WORD ENDINGS

Open index.html from the complete Word-Endings folder. Keep all files and assets together. No build command is needed. This is an unzipped local project; it has not been published.

ENTRY AND LEVELS
Choose one of 20 school-supply picture buttons. No name, student ID, password or sign-in is needed. The chosen supply stays visible during activities and identifies its records in Excel.
There are 450 picture cards from the five supplied posters, 158 pictured vocabulary words and 2,700 activities across A, B, C, AA, BB and CC.
A alternates 225 whole-word cloze questions with 225 word-ending questions. Each has one blank and three choices.
All levels spread ending families throughout the activity order instead of following poster order. The nine common families are mixed from the opening questions; duplicate pictures of a word are spaced within its family. Shuffle activities also keeps the ending mix. Existing profiles retain their current activity, answers, assessment history and balloon score when the order updates.
B and C have one missing word ending and three choices. C adds a connected detail about the picture.
AA has two blanks, BB three, and CC four, with five reusable choices. These levels mix missing whole words with missing endings: AA has 150 mixed questions, BB 300, and all 450 CC questions are mixed. There are 900 whole-word blanks across these levels.
Mixed banks contain two whole words and three ending pieces; ending-only banks contain five ending pieces. The active-blank label says CHOOSE A WHOLE WORD or CHOOSE A WORD ENDING.

WORD ENDINGS, PICTURES AND HIGHLIGHTS
Ending buttons show actual final letters. Examples: ow in snow, own in crown, ay in play, ight in night, ound in ground, ake in cake, ain in train, eep in sheep, ore in shore, and ing in spring.
The additional words from the supplied posters retain their actual endings, including an in snowman, ake in snowflake, on in crayon, eight in weight, out in roundabout and oo in igloo. In total there are 39 final spelling chunks.
These spelling chunks are not always grammatical suffixes. The same letters can have different sounds: cow and snow; a ribbon bow and bowing your head; mountain and again; weight and height. The sentence voice models the complete word.
Coral highlights mark the final ending on complete vocabulary words and ending pieces. An unfinished prefix beside an empty blank is not highlighted as a separate complete word. Bank letters remain grey; placed answers and joined words are black.
Text uses upright Century Gothic where installed, with a sans-serif fallback on other devices.
The picture captions have been cropped away. Embedded answer text on the store sign, May calendar, beep pictures, mistake worksheet and labelled weights is covered in the saved assets as well as the interface.
Mixed poster headings do not determine the answer. The actual word does: crown ends in own, snowflake in ake, and creek in eek. Incorrect source captions are corrected: the extra cushion picture is pillow; the bird labelled dow is duck; the snowy shelter labelled snowbank is igloo. The unlabelled bison uses bison.
Picture-specific wording distinguishes an archery bow, a tied ribbon and a boy bowing; bubbles from blowing wind; snow clearing from ploughing soil; the snail and turtle marked slow; a ball and a round pizza; an hourglass and clock; compounds made of buildings or atoms; a gavel, weight and boxing glove; and a plane or paper plane in flight.
Each vocabulary subject has connected follow-up details. Questions do not repeat generic filler such as I see it, point to it or I can say its name. The longest question has 29 words.
Picture crops stay at or below their native width.

NOTEBOOK, ANSWERS AND VOICE
The new colour scheme uses navy and coral on a pale sky background. The notebook keeps cream paper, red margins, pencil and ruled writing rows. Lines sit below the words.
The date follows the current local device calendar, for example Thursday October 15th, 2026. It updates at midnight and when returning to the project.
Tap the active blank and then a button, or drag a button onto any blank. Choices remain in the bank for reuse. Correct answers select the next unfinished blank. Incorrect answers stay visible so the student can try another choice.
The student's answer appears within the sentence. MY JOINED WORDS separately shows the whole word each current answer forms, including mistaken combinations. Clear this blank, Reset question, navigation and reload restoration keep both displays consistent. Empty blanks never reveal the answer.
Listen speaks only the full target sentence or connected sentences. Joining words, selecting answers, highlighting and balloon rewards add no speech. Stop cancels speech. The device's English voice and the chosen voice speed are used.
Three Next buttons are provided: after the date, beside the choices and at the bottom.
Reset question clears current answers and hints and stops speech while keeping assessment history and completed totals. Teacher tools has a separate confirmed reset for the selected school supply.
Layout density adapts to available height. Very small screens can scroll to preserve readable text and touch targets. Live browser/tablet layout and real-device gestures remain unverified because local preview is blocked in this session.

KIDS IN THE BALLOON
The exact supplied rainbow balloon PNG with four children is reused without generating new artwork. Its transparency and original colours are preserved.
The established rising behaviour is reused. One balloon rises vertically in a reserved strip at the right, and the cumulative count appears on the basket.
Each question first completed correctly earns one step. Every blank must be correct together. Wrong choices, individual blanks, repeated checks and redoing an already completed question do not add points.
At 20 correct questions the balloon reaches the top, then restarts at the bottom after a short pause. The cumulative count continues through 20, 21, 40, 41 and beyond. Only one balloon is shown at a time.
The score is saved per school supply across all six levels and restores after reload. Question reset keeps the score; full profile reset clears only that supply's score. Quick answers cannot trigger an old flight's reset timer. Reduced-motion settings disable animation.

EXCEL AND STORAGE
Teacher tools on the activity page includes Excel assessment for the current school supply. You do not have to return to the landing page. The landing page's Excel assessment exports all objects.
The real .xlsx workbook has Summary, Progress, Blanks, Attempts and Guide sheets. There is no PDF assessment.
Progress records total answer attempts per question. Blanks records attempts and correct selections per blank. Attempts records every tapped or dragged answer, its actual target word, correct ending or whole word, chosen answer, correctness, offered buttons, current sentence, input method, listens, hints, teacher support and active time. Check my answer is counted separately.
The current-supply export contains all 2,700 activities and 5,400 blanks. The all-objects export contains summaries for all 20 supplies and detailed records for activities already started.
The selected school supply appears on every assessment row. Two students choosing the same supply share that profile.
Saved work uses the independent word-endings-v1 browser-storage key. Earlier projects keep their own files and progress. Work stays in the current browser on the current device. Export before switching devices or clearing browser data. An Excel export is an assessment record, not a restore file.
Tap, mouse, finger and stylus input are supported through Pointer Events. Pull-to-refresh is suppressed in the activity work area while the teacher menu remains scrollable. Browser toolbar and operating-system refresh controls remain available; saved answers restore after reload.

VERIFICATION
Automated data and simulated-interface checks cover all activities and answer spans, final-ending highlights, whole-word choices, three/five-choice banks, separate joined words, tap and pointer placement, voice and Stop, dates, resets, saved profiles, all Next buttons and refresh-gesture suppression.
Separate balloon checks cover 20/40-question transitions, cumulative totals, reloads, profile resets and quick-answer timer handling. Excel files are independently opened and checked using openpyxl. Native picture contact sheets are inspected and caption edges corrected.
