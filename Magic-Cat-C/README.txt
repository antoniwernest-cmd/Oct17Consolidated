Magic Cat C - Halloween

Open ../Magic-Cat/index.html to choose Magic Cat B or C, or open this index.html directly. Keep all three folders together with their assets folders. No ID or sign-in is needed. No ZIP is needed.

125 illustrated scenes from the linked Mystic Halloween Cat activity. The picture descriptions have been checked against the artwork. Pictures are extracted without enlargement.
Six levels: A, AA, B, BB, C, CC. Two distinct activities per scene in each level.
250 activities per level; 1,500 total.
A and B use one blank; C uses two; AA uses three; BB uses five; CC uses seven.

Same features as Magic Cat B: phonics colours in reading text and answer choices, a colour key, selected-word explanations, highlighting on/off, Listen and Stop speaking, Back and Next, Mix, Retry, assessment scores, saved progress, PDF assessment through printing, and Excel-compatible CSV assessment. No ID is requested or included in assessment exports.

Landing message: 1. It reads to me. 2. It highlights text features. 3. It is leveled.

Magic Cat C saves progress separately from Magic Cat B and the original Halloween activity. Listening uses an English voice available on your device. Pronunciations may vary by accent.
