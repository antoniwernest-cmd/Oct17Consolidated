Magic Cat B - refreshed picture cards and sound colours

Open ../Magic-Cat/index.html to choose Magic Cat B or C, or open this index.html directly. Keep all three folders together with their assets folders. No ID or sign-in is needed. No ZIP is needed.

Six levels: A, AA, B, BB, C, CC.
520 activities per level; 3,120 total.
260 supplied cards: 160 food cards from the ten-page PDF and 100 scenes from the four new affirmation sheets.
Two distinct activities for every picture in every level.
A and B: one missing word. C: two missing words.
AA: three blanks. BB: five blanks. CC: seven blanks.

Source artwork is extracted into individual PNG files without enlargement or sharpening. The original separate captions are excluded so the missing words are not given away. Text printed inside the original artwork remains. Pictures display at or below the source pixel width.

Sound colours appear in reading text and answer choices. Blends, consonant teams, vowels, vowel neighbours, magic e, r vowels, endings, tricky spellings, and silent letters have distinct colours and marks. Meaningful suffixes also have a green underline, so their sound colour remains visible. Select a word to see its explanation; use Sound colours to turn highlighting off or on. A plain consonant stays plain when no special pattern applies. Pronunciations can vary by accent.

All original level buttons, listening, stop speaking, mix, retry, assessment, and reset controls remain. This card edition stores progress separately from earlier editions to prevent old scores attaching to different pictures.
PDF assessment opens the print dialog; choose Save as PDF. Excel assessment downloads a CSV. Read-aloud uses available English voices on your device.
