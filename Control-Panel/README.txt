CONTROL PANEL

Open index.html from the complete Control-Panel folder. The project runs locally with no build command. Keep all JavaScript, CSS and assets together. This delivery is an unzipped folder; nothing has been published.

ENTRY AND LEVELS
Choose one of 20 school-supply picture buttons. No student ID, name, password or sign-in is required. The selected supply appears on every activity and is recorded in Excel. Each supply has its own saved answers, teacher ratings and score.
There are 400 picture cards, 135 pictured vocabulary words and 2,400 activities across A, B, C, AA, BB and CC. Multiple supplied pictures can show the same word.
A alternates 200 whole-word cloze questions with 200 missing-group questions. Each question has one blank and three choices.
B and C have one r-controlled group blank and three choices. C adds a connected detail about the picture.
AA has two blanks, BB three, and CC four. These levels have five reusable r-controlled group buttons. Each follow-up sentence develops the pictured subject rather than repeating generic filler.

SPELLING AND HIGHLIGHTS
The main button groups are ar, er, ir, or and ur. Longer spellings stay together: eer in deer; oor in door; ore in shore, store, snore and explore. The or in forest remains or.
Peach highlights ar; blue highlights er and eer; lavender highlights ir; gold highlights or, oor and ore; pink highlights ur. Highlights appear in visible vocabulary, answer choices, filled blanks and MY JOINED WORDS.
The same spelling can have different sounds or stress in different words, including water, world and worm. The device voice models the complete word within its sentence.
Tap the active blank and then a choice, or drag a button onto any blank. Buttons remain available for reuse. Correct answers select the next unfinished blank. Wrong choices stay visible for another try.
The empty blank does not show the answer. When an answer is placed, it appears within the sentence and in a separate joined-word display. Incorrect combinations are shown too, so the display does not reveal the correct word before the student supplies it.
Bank letters are grey; placed answers and joined words are black. Text is upright Century Gothic where installed, with a sans-serif fallback for devices without that font.

NOTEBOOK AND VOICE
The notebook has cream paper, red margins and a pencil. Fixed-height writing rows place the ruling below the words. The interface uses the available screen width while reserving room for the score balloon.
The date follows the current local device calendar, for example Wednesday October 14th, 2026. It refreshes at midnight and when returning to the project.
Listen speaks only the full target sentence or connected sentences. Choosing answers, joining words and earning rewards add no speech. Stop cancels speech, and Voice speed changes the reading pace.
Three Next buttons are provided: after the date, beside the answer bank and at the bottom. They advance the same question and disable at the end of a level.
Reset question clears current answers, hides the hint and stops speech while preserving assessment history and completed-question totals. Teacher tools has a separate confirmed reset for all progress belonging to the selected supply.

BALLOON REWARD
One smiling balloon rises vertically in a strip at the right. Each question first completed correctly earns one step. All blanks must be correct together. Partial answers, wrong choices, repeated checks and revisiting a completed activity do not earn extra points.
At 20 correct questions, the balloon reaches the top; a new-colour balloon starts at the bottom after a short pause. Only one balloon is shown at a time. The cumulative count continues through 20, 21, 40, 41 and onward.
Scores span all six levels within the selected school supply. They restore after reloading. Question reset preserves the score; full object reset clears only that supply's score. Reduced-motion settings disable animated movement.
The balloon artwork is reused from Neighbour Master. It was previously created by editing the supplied smiling-balloon illustration with the built-in image-generation tool.

PICTURES
Cards are native-resolution crops from the five supplied r-controlled vowel posters. Printed vocabulary captions have been cropped away. Embedded answer text on the term calendar and store sign is covered in the saved image itself and in the interface.
Picture-specific wording distinguishes a dog barking from tree bark, the pitchfork from a table fork, playing cards from greeting cards, a bird perching from a perch fish, a whirl symbol from a tornado, burnt toast from a fire, and a skater turning from a direction arrow.
Mixed poster columns are corrected using each word's actual spelling. Examples: nurse uses ur, bird uses ir, and burger uses ur. The pitchfork labelled farm is taught as fork; the computer labelled server is taught as computer.
Pictures that do not demonstrate an appropriate r-controlled spelling or do not match their caption have been omitted, including run, lunch, the empty nest and pirate. No new unrelated picture assets have been substituted.
Pictures are not enlarged beyond their native width.

TABLETS, STORAGE AND EXCEL
Finger, stylus and mouse dragging use Pointer Events. Tap buttons also work. Pull-to-refresh gestures are suppressed in the activity while the teacher menu stays scrollable. Browser toolbar refresh and operating-system controls remain available. Saved answers restore after reload.
The layout adapts to screen height and keeps readable text and touch targets. Very small screens may scroll. Live browser/tablet appearance and real-device gestures remain unverified because local preview was blocked in this session.
Control Panel saves only to this browser on this device under control-panel-v1, independently of the earlier projects. Two students choosing the same supply share that profile. Export before changing devices or clearing browser data.
Teacher tools downloads a real .xlsx workbook for the selected supply. The landing page exports all 20 supplies. Sheets: Summary, Progress, Blanks, Attempts and Guide. There is no PDF assessment.
Excel records the supply, level, activity, pictured word, actual blank word, correct group or whole word, chosen answer, offered buttons, correctness, sentence, input method, listens, hints, checks, teacher support and active time. Whole-word questions and each r-controlled spelling group are identified separately. Header cells use the project's plum colour.
The current-supply export includes all 2,400 activities and 4,800 blanks. The all-supplies export includes summaries for all 20 supplies and detailed rows for activities already started. An export is an assessment record, not a restore file.

VERIFICATION
Automated data and simulated-interface checks cover all 2,400 activities, exact word and sentence assembly, every spelling group, choice counts, whole-word cloze, highlights, joined words, tap and pointer placement, sentence-only speech, Stop, dates, resets, saved work, independent profiles, three Next buttons and refresh-gesture suppression.
Balloon checks cover the 20/40-question transitions, cumulative scoring, quick-next race handling, reloading and profile resets. Excel downloads are independently opened and checked with openpyxl. Native picture contact sheets were inspected and crop edges corrected.
