NEIGHBOUR MASTER

Open index.html, or upload the complete Neighbour-Master folder to a static host. Keep the JavaScript, CSS and assets together. No build command is needed. This is an unzipped local project; nothing has been published.

ENTRY AND LEVELS
The project keeps Compound Master's school-supply entry page, notebook, red margins, pencil, dynamic date, Next buttons, saved work and Excel assessment. Choose one of 20 school-supply pictures. No name, ID or password is required. The selected picture remains visible during questions and appears in the assessment.
There are 348 picture questions per level and 2,088 activities altogether, using 151 pictured vocabulary words. The same word can appear in different supplied pictures.
A: 174 whole-word cloze questions alternating with 174 vowel-pattern questions. Each has one blank and three matching choices; whole-word questions have whole-word buttons.
B and C: one vowel-pattern blank and three choices. Sentences grow in length.
AA: two blanks. BB: three blanks. CC: four blanks. These levels have five reusable vowel-pattern choices. Follow-up sentences develop the same pictured subject: for example, rain makes a road wet, fills pools and helps seeds grow. Each of the 151 vocabulary subjects has its own connected details. C includes the first follow-up detail with one blank; AA, BB and CC add one, two and three follow-up details with additional vowel-pattern blanks.

ANSWERS, HIGHLIGHTS AND VOICE
Tap a blank and then a choice, or drag a button onto any blank. Choices remain available for reuse. Correct answers select the next unfinished blank. Incorrect choices stay visible so the student can try again.
Yellow highlights vowel neighbours in visible sentence text, the answer bank, filled blanks and the separate MY JOINED WORDS display. Purple marks the supplied igh group; teal marks the supplied r-vowel patterns in their vocabulary words. Bank text stays grey, placed text stays black, and all lettering is upright Century Gothic with a sans-serif fallback.
A missing group is not printed in its empty blank. Once a student supplies an answer, the separate display shows the whole word that their answer forms. It includes mistaken combinations, rather than revealing the target beforehand. Several blanks produce several completed-word displays. Clear this blank, Reset question, navigation and saved-work restoration update this display.
Listen reads only the complete target sentence. Joining words, highlights and rewards add no spoken instructions or score announcements. Stop cancels speech. Voice speed can be changed. The device's built-in English voice is used.
Words and blanks stay within fixed-height notebook writing rows. Blue ruling sits below the letters. The date follows the current local device calendar, for example Wednesday October 14th, 2026, and refreshes at midnight and when returning to the project.
Next buttons are after the year, beside the answer bank and at the bottom. They advance together and disable at the end of a level. Reset question clears current answers and hints while preserving assessment history and completed-question totals. Teacher tools has a separate confirmed reset for all work belonging to the selected school supply.

BALLOON REWARD
One smiling balloon rises vertically in a reserved strip at the right of the activity. It starts at the bottom and moves up one step for each question first completed correctly. All blanks must be correct together to earn a step. Individual blanks, wrong choices, repeated checks and revisiting a completed question do not earn extra steps.
After 20 correct questions, the balloon reaches the top. A new-colour balloon starts at the bottom after a short pause. Only one balloon is shown at a time, and its cumulative score keeps increasing through 20, 21, 40, 41 and onward. A quick next answer cannot trigger an old balloon's reset timer.
Totals are saved per school supply across all six levels and restore after reload. Question reset preserves the total; full profile reset clears only that school supply. The balloon sways while rising and rests between advances. Reduced-motion settings disable animated movement.
The balloon strip reserves its own space so it does not cover the notebook. Layout density adapts to available height. Very small screens can scroll to keep text and touch targets readable. Live browser/tablet appearance remains unverified because local preview is blocked in this session.

CONTENT AND PICTURES
Pictures are native crops of the four supplied vowel-neighbour posters. Their printed word captions are cropped away. Embedded text on glue bottles, soap, red new tags and the zoo sign is covered with overlays that resize with the image. Pictures are not enlarged beyond their native width.
Button groups include ai, ay, ee, ea, oa, oo, oi, ow, ou, ue, ie, oe, ui, au, aw, oy and ew. The supplied igh spelling and r-vowel groups ar, or, ir, er and ur are also included, with distinct highlight colours and assessment categories. These spellings can represent different sounds in different words; the sentence voice models each complete word.
Entries that only demonstrate a single y or a split magic-e spelling, such as fly, sky, cry, dry, bike, kite, slide, rope, flute and cube, are excluded from this group-button activity. The supplied fruit picture uses ui. Labels and sentences match each pictured vocabulary item.

TABLET USE, STORAGE AND EXCEL
Finger, stylus and mouse dragging use Pointer Events; tap buttons work without dragging. Pull-to-refresh gestures are suppressed in the activity while the teacher menu remains scrollable. Browser toolbar refresh and operating-system controls remain browser-controlled. Saved answers restore after reload.
Neighbour Master uses its own neighbour-master-v1 storage key and preserves the other projects. Work stays in this browser on this device. Two children selecting the same school supply share that profile. Export before changing devices or clearing browser data. An export is an assessment record, not a restore file.
The connected-sentence revision preserves completed-question totals, teacher ratings and assessment history. When a revised question is first opened, earlier partial answers and per-blank counts are cleared so they cannot fill different words by mistake. New partial work then saves normally. Older attempt records retain their original words, choices and sentences.
Teacher tools downloads a real .xlsx workbook for the current school supply. The entry page can export all 20 objects. Sheets: Summary, Progress, Blanks, Attempts and Guide. There is no PDF assessment.
Excel records the school supply, level, question, actual target word, answer category, correct choice, student choice, offered buttons, correctness, current sentence, tap/drag method, listens, hints, checks, teacher support and active time. Whole-word questions and vowel-pattern questions are identified separately. Highlights are visual only and do not add HTML to assessment values.

BALLOON ARTWORK
Saved asset: assets/balloon.png. Created using the built-in image-generation tool by editing the supplied smiling-balloon picture. Only one red balloon and its ribbon are retained; CSS hue rotation changes the colour for new flights. Original tool outputs are retained in the generated-images folder.

Keep the balloon's cheerful face, glossy red material, shape, highlights and 3D illustration style. Remove all five other balloons, all sky and all clouds. Show the complete red balloon with a short curling ribbon, on a tall tightly framed approximately 2:3 transparent canvas; the balloon body should occupy about the upper 70 percent and the ribbon the lower 30 percent. Keep a little breathing room around the entire subject. Do not crop the balloon or ribbon. Leave a small blank red area below the smile and above the knot for the website to overlay the student's changing score. No text, numbers, extra objects, shadows, haze or coloured background. All pixels outside the balloon and its ribbon must be fully transparent.
Final transparency-edit prompt: Use case: background-extraction. Correct ONLY the transparency of this single red balloon sprite. Keep the exact same red smiling balloon, tied neck and curling ribbon; do not redesign, rescale or move them. Remove every bit of the surrounding red glow, red haze, shadow, dark background and coloured backdrop. Outside the crisp silhouette of the balloon and the thin curling ribbon, every pixel must have alpha zero.
