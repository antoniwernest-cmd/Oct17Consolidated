SHORT VOWELS

Open index.html from the complete Short-Vowels folder. Keep the files and assets together. No build command is needed. This is an unzipped local project.

ACTIVITIES
Choose one of 20 school-supply pictures to begin or resume. No name, student ID, password or sign-in is required. Each supply has its own saved work and cumulative score.
There are 110 native picture cards, 58 pictured words and 660 activities across A, B, C, AA, BB and CC. All five short vowels are mixed throughout every level and after Shuffle activities.
A alternates missing-vowel questions with whole-word cloze: one blank and three choices. There are 55 of each type.
B and C have one missing short vowel and three vowel choices. C adds a connected sentence about the picture.
AA has two blanks, BB three and CC four, with five reusable choices. These retain the established mixture of whole-word cloze and missing letters: 36 mixed AA questions, 73 mixed BB questions and all 110 CC questions. Mixed banks have two whole words and three vowels; vowel-only higher-level banks have all five vowels.
Vowel buttons contain a, e, i, o or u. Drag a button onto any blank or select a blank and tap a button. The active label distinguishes CHOOSE A SHORT VOWEL from CHOOSE A WHOLE WORD. Choices stay in the bank for reuse.
All missing-vowel targets are three-letter words with a short vowel in the middle: cat, bed, pig, log and sun are examples. Golden highlights mark the vowel in complete target words and selected vowel letters. Empty blanks never show the answer. Bank letters are grey and placed answers are black.
MY JOINED WORDS separately displays the whole word formed by each current answer, including incorrect combinations. It updates after placement, clearing, reset and reload.
Follow-up sentences develop the picture's subject. They do not use repeated generic filler. The longest activity has 26 words.

PICTURE ACCURACY
Native crops of the supplied posters retain their clarity; printed captions are removed. BUS and TAXI text inside pictures is covered in both the saved assets and interface.
The duplicate Set 9 attachment is included once. The jar is excluded because ar is r-controlled. The yo-yo labelled yog is excluded because yog is not a suitable short-vowel word.
Mislabelled or ambiguous pictures use accurate targets: sit for the child sitting in a wagon, rug for the rolled rug, rag for the cleaning cloth, leg for the boy holding his sore knee, and man for the adult giving a thumbs up. The red target refers to the hen's red comb; fin refers to the fish's fin; pet works for both pictured pets.
The two cloth-cleaning mop pictures use picture-specific wording about mopping up water with a cloth. Short-vowel highlights are limited to the actual known target words and vowel choices.

NOTEBOOK AND VOICE
The colour scheme uses indigo, sky blue, rocket red and gold highlights. Cream notebook paper, red margin, pencil, upright Century Gothic text and rules below each writing row are retained.
The full date follows the local device calendar, including the ordinal day, and updates at midnight and on returning to the app.
Listen reads only the complete target sentence or connected sentences. Joining words and rocket rewards add no speech. Stop cancels speech. The device's English voice and selected speed are used.
Next buttons are after the date, beside the choices and in the footer. All three share the same navigation state.
Reset question clears current answers and the hint display while preserving attempts, completion history, teacher rating and score. Teacher tools has a separate confirmed reset for the selected school-supply profile.
Pointer Events support mouse, touch and stylus dragging. Pull-to-refresh is suppressed in the activity work area while the teacher menu remains scrollable. Browser and operating-system refresh controls remain available.
Layout density adapts to screen height; very small screens can scroll to preserve readable text and touch targets.

ROCKET REWARD
The existing balloon rising logic is reused with the supplied four-children rocket artwork. One rocket rises vertically in a reserved strip on the right. Its body badge shows the cumulative count.
One first fully correct question earns one step. All blanks must be correct together. Wrong choices, individual blanks, repeated checks and redoing completed questions earn no extra points.
At 20 correct questions the rocket reaches the top, pauses briefly, and a new rocket starts at the bottom with the cumulative total. This continues through 20, 21, 40, 41 and beyond. Only one rocket is shown at a time.
The total is saved per school supply across all six levels. Reloading restores the total and flight position. A quick next answer cannot trigger an old flight's reset timer. Reduced-motion settings disable animation.

EXCEL ASSESSMENT
Teacher tools on the activity page includes Excel assessment for the current school supply. Returning to the landing page is not necessary. The landing-page export includes all 20 supplies.
The real .xlsx workbook has Summary, Progress, Blanks, Attempts and Guide sheets. Progress counts attempts per question. Blanks counts attempts and correct selections per blank. Attempts logs every tap/drop with its target word, correct vowel or whole word, selected answer, correctness, offered choices, current sentence, input method, hints, listens, teacher support and active time. Sentence checks are recorded separately.
The current-supply export includes all 660 activities and 1,320 blanks. The all-objects export includes summaries for all 20 supplies and detailed records of started activities. Each assessment row identifies the school supply.
Saved work uses the independent short-vowels-v1 browser-storage key. Earlier projects keep their own files and progress. Records stay in this browser on this device. Export before clearing browser data or switching devices. Excel is an assessment record, not a restore file.
